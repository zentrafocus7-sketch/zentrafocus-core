**Title:** Marketing Channels Policy
**Purpose:** Defines which channels ZentraFocus runs, the standing rules for each, and the conditions under which paid traffic is allowed — so channel decisions don't get relitigated per campaign.
**Policy:** Active channels: TikTok (primary growth engine), Pinterest, Kit email, Blog, Reddit. Content follows a TOFU/MOFU/BOFU funnel structure throughout. TikTok: the "V5 Broken" structure (single-word open, broken sentences, echo close) is the highest-performing format found to date and is the default structure until evidence says otherwise (per constitution/PRINCIPLES.md, Principle 12 — no change without a stated reason). Pinterest: maximum 5 power hashtags per pin (not 15), keywords placed in the first three words of the title and in the opening sentence of the description, and descriptions must preserve the curiosity gap rather than over-explaining. SEO: priority keyword is "how to stop procrastinating" (~165K monthly searches); all blog authority consolidates on zentrafocus.app/blog/ — a single domain, never split across properties. Paid advertising is not run until the account has at least 500 followers and the funnel has been validated organically; once resumed, the ad objective is profile visits, not video views. Every channel operates under domains/brand/VOICE.md and domains/brand/IDENTITY.md — channel format may adapt, brand voice does not.
