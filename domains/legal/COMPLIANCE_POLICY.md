**Title:** Compliance Policy
**Purpose:** Defines ZentraFocus's standing refund and data-handling rules, and points to where the binding legal text lives — the single reference so these terms are never restated or allowed to drift between the website, product pages, and support replies.
**Policy:** Refund: every paid product carries a 30-day, no-questions-asked full refund. The window is fixed at 30 days and is not shortened, lengthened, or made conditional until evidence demonstrates it is causing material harm to the business (constitution/PRINCIPLES.md, Principle 12 — no rule change without a stated reason). Data handling: ZentraFocus collects only what is needed to deliver products and email communications — an email address via Kit (registry/systems/kit.md) and purchase details via Gumroad (registry/systems/gumroad.md). Personal data is never sold to third parties. Tracking is limited to the Meta Pixel for ad measurement (registry/systems/meta-ads.md) and standard hosting-level analytics (registry/systems/hosting.md). The binding legal text — Privacy Policy and Terms of Service — is published live at zentrafocus.app/privacy/ and zentrafocus.app/terms/ and is not duplicated here (registry/repositories/legal-pages.md); Core governs the rule, the website holds the record of it.
