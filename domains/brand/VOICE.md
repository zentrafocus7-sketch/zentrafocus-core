**Title:** Brand Voice Policy
**Purpose:** Defines how ZentraFocus communicates, in any channel, so voice stays recognizable regardless of who or what is writing it — founder, contractor, or AI agent.
**Policy:** Voice is clinical, direct, evidence-based, and anti-motivational. Content is grounded in neuroscience and evidence-based framing rather than hype or generic inspiration. Writing addresses the reader directly, without fluff, without hedging, and without the vague uplift language typical of the productivity/self-help category. Never: hype, generic motivational language, vague inspiration, or "you can do it" framing. Always: direct address, evidence-based reasoning, and the brand's standing philosophy (per domains/brand/IDENTITY.md) that architecture — not motivation — is what changes behavior. This applies uniformly across TikTok, Pinterest, email, blog, and Reddit; channel format may change, voice does not.
