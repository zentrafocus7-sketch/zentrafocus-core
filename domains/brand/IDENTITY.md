**Title:** Brand Identity Policy
**Purpose:** Defines who ZentraFocus is for and what it stands for, so every product, campaign, and asset stays aligned to one identity instead of drifting per channel or per contributor.
**Policy:** ZentraFocus (zentrafocus.app) is a neuroscience-backed focus and productivity brand. Positioning: "Systems. Not Inspiration." and "Architecture beats willpower." Audience: US knowledge workers, students, and creators — primarily male, 18–34 — struggling with dopamine dysregulation, digital distraction, and attention fragmentation. Brand philosophy rejects motivational and inspirational framing entirely; focus and productivity are treated as a systems and architecture problem, never a willpower or discipline problem. Every product, piece of content, or campaign must be checkable against this positioning before it ships — if it reads as generic motivational content, it does not belong to this brand, regardless of channel.
