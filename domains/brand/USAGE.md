**Title:** Brand Usage Policy
**Purpose:** Defines how identity, voice, and visual policy get enforced in practice across every downstream surface, so alignment doesn't depend on any one person remembering the rules.
**Policy:** Any new product, campaign, or asset is checked against domains/brand/IDENTITY.md and domains/brand/VOICE.md before it ships — not after. Visual assets pull their values from design-system/ directly; nothing independently restates a color or typeface. Voice and positioning stay identical across every channel (TikTok, Pinterest, Kit email, Blog, Reddit); only format adapts per channel, never tone or substance. Where a channel-specific rule exists (for example, Pinterest's hashtag and keyword conventions in domains/marketing/CHANNELS.md), that rule governs execution details only — it never overrides identity or voice.
