**Title:** Legal & Compliance Domain Added
**Type:** Architectural
**Decision:** Add a fifth domain, `domains/legal/`, governing refund terms and data-handling rules, and register the already-live Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages (zentrafocus.app/privacy/, zentrafocus.app/terms/) in `registry/repositories/`. Justified under constitution/BLUEPRINT.md Section 7: the domain has exactly one nameable owner (Founder, Domain Owner Level 2); it covers scope no existing domain covers (Product explicitly excludes fulfillment; Marketing explicitly excludes execution and legal disclosure); and it resolves a real, current ambiguity, not a speculative one — a live 30-day refund policy and a live privacy policy already govern real transactions today with no corresponding record or owner inside Core, in tension with Principle 2 (Single Point of Truth) and Principle 7 (Nothing Is True Until It Is Recorded).

**Approved By:** Founder (Level 0)
